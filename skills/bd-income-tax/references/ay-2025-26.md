# AY 2025-26: parameters (prior year, for late/prior returns)

**Income year:** 1 Jul 2024 to 30 Jun 2025 → **Assessment Year 2025-26**.
**Governing law:** Income Tax Act 2023 as amended by the **Finance Ordinance 2025** (enacted
by the interim government with Parliament dissolved). `[ITA-2023]` `[FO-2025]`

Slabs, thresholds, minimum tax and the rebate formula below were checked against the NBR
Income Tax Guide (Nirdeshika) 2025-26 `[NBR-Guide-2025]`.

All figures are in BDT. Numbers feed `scripts/tax_calc.py`. Never hand-calculate.

## General individual slab `[FO-2025]` `[NBR-Guide-2025]`

| Income band | Cumulative upper bound | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| First 350,000 | 350,000 | 0% |
| Next 100,000 | 450,000 | 5% |
| Next 400,000 | 850,000 | 10% |
| Next 500,000 | 1,350,000 | 15% |
| Next 500,000 | 1,850,000 | 20% |
| Next 2,000,000 | 3,850,000 | 25% |
| Balance | n/a | 30% |

> The **5% slab still applies** this year (abolished from AY 2026-27). At the same income,
> AY 2025-26 tax differs from AY 2026-27: total income 880,000 gives **49,500** here and
> **57,000** under AY 2026-27.

The slab widths apply to income **above** the taxpayer's tax-free threshold below.

## Tax-free thresholds by category `[FO-2025]` `[NBR-Guide-2025]`

| Category (`tax_calc.py` key) | Threshold |
|---|---|
| General (`general`) | 350,000 |
| Women; anyone aged 65 or older (`female` / `senior`) | 400,000 |
| Third-gender; persons with disabilities (`third_gender` / `disabled`) | 475,000 |
| Gazetted war-wounded freedom fighters (`freedom_fighter`) | 500,000 |
| Parent or legal guardian of a person with a disability (`--disabled-children N`) | + 50,000 per child/dependant |

There is **no separate July-fighter threshold this year**; that category starts in AY 2026-27,
and the calculator rejects `july_warrior` for AY 2025-26.

## Salary exemption (employment head) `[ITA-2023]`

Exempt the **lower of** one-third of total salary income or **BDT 450,000**.

## Exempt end-of-service benefits `[ITA-2023-6Sch]`

Same as AY 2026-27: **gratuity** from a government or NBR-approved fund is exempt up to
**BDT 2.5 crore**; the excess is taxable. See `ay-2026-27.md`.

## Investment tax rebate: Section 78 `[NBR-Guide-2025]`

The rebate is the **lowest** of:

1. 3% of total income (same exclusions as AY 2026-27);
2. **15%** of total eligible investment;
3. **BDT 1,000,000**.

DPS counts up to BDT 120,000 a year and government securities up to BDT 500,000.

**No rebate if the return was filed after Tax Day** (`--filed-late`). The tax is then computed
without deducting any rebate.

## Minimum tax (area-based, last year of this system) `[FO-2025]` `[NBR-Guide-2025]`

| Location (`tax_calc.py` key) | Minimum tax |
|---|---|
| Dhaka North, Dhaka South, Chattogram City Corporation (`dhaka_ctg_cc`) | 5,000 |
| Other city corporations (`other_cc`) | 4,000 |
| Outside city corporations (`other`) | 3,000 |
| New taxpayers (`--new-taxpayer`) | 1,000 |

The location is where the taxpayer lived for most of the income year. The minimum applies
whenever income exceeds the threshold, even if the rebate brings the computed tax to zero.

**Gross-receipts floor:** the calculator keeps the rule carried from earlier releases (0.25% of
gross receipts above BDT 40,000,000). ⚠️ unverified: it was not re-checked against the
AY 2025-26 text. Confirm before relying on it.

## Tax Day and late filing

- Tax Day for this year was **30 November 2025**. ⚠️ unverified: the NBR extended the date;
  confirm the final extended date before deciding whether a return was late.
- A return filed after Tax Day lost the investment rebate (`--filed-late`).
- A return for this year filed after 30 June 2026 falls under the amended Section 174 (see
  `filing.md`). ⚠️ unverified how the old and new rules combine for such a return.

## Net-wealth surcharge

Same bands as AY 2026-27 (see `ay-2026-27.md`). `[NBR-Guide-2025]`

## Other heads and rules

Dividends were taxed at slab rates this year (use `--dividend`; the calculator adds them to
regular income), and gold or silver held personally was not yet a capital asset. Other heads
follow the structure in `ay-2026-27.md`. `[ITA-2023]`
