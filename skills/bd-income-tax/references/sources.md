# Sources

Every rate/threshold in the reference files is tagged with one of the source keys below
(e.g. `[ITA-2023]`, `[FA-2026]`, `[NBR-Guide-2026]`). Always cite the source for a figure when
you present it to a professional or on request.

The FY 2026-27 measures are the **Finance Act 2026** (Act No. 96 of 2026), gazetted on
30 June 2026. The AY 2026-27 figures in this skill were checked against the gazetted Act and
the NBR's own Income Tax Guide for the year. Still tell users to confirm against current NBR
circulars before filing: SROs and special orders can change details mid-year.

## Primary / official

- **`[FA-2026]`** Finance Act 2026 (অর্থ আইন, ২০২৬), Act No. 96 of 2026, Bangladesh Gazette
  Extraordinary, 30 Jun 2026. Schedule 2 Part 1 = individual tax rates for AY 2026-27 to
  AY 2030-31 (paras Ka, Kha, Ga); Part 2 = surcharge; Part 3 = environmental surcharge.
  Amends ITA 2023 sections 78 (rebate), 153 (vehicle advance tax), 154 (advance tax), 162
  (delay interest), 163 (refundable source tax, turnover tax), 170 and 174 (return timing).
  `https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/acts/Finance_Act_2026.pdf`
- **`[NBR-Guide-2026]`** NBR Income Tax Guide (আয়কর নির্দেশিকা) 2026-2027, published Sep 2026.
  Worked examples used as calculator test cases: tax tables (p.56), disabled-child threshold
  (example 17, p.57), minimum tax and rebate rule (pp.58-59), rebate examples 18-19
  (pp.61-63) and the new-taxpayer case (p.67), incentive and additional tax (example 23,
  pp.68-70), surcharge (pp.71-74), vehicle advance tax and environmental surcharge (examples
  24-25, pp.74-78), late return (example 26, pp.78-82), business owner (example Kha,
  pp.103-105). Appendix 5 = e-Return User Manual; Appendix 6 = e-Return FAQ.
  `https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/publications/Aykor_Nirdashikha_2026-2027_BG_Press_Modify_Copy.pdf`
- **`[NBR-Guide-2025]`** NBR Income Tax Guide 2025-2026 (AY 2025-26 slabs and thresholds p.51,
  area-based minimum tax p.52, rebate rule p.53, Tax Day rebate forfeiture p.59).
  `https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/publications/Nirdeshika_2025-26.pdf`
- **`[SO-1-2026]`** NBR Special Order 1/2026 (28 Jun 2026): mandatory online return filing for
  individuals and its exceptions. Reproduced in `[NBR-Guide-2026]` p.6.
- **`[ITA-2023]`** Income Tax Act 2023 (আয়কর আইন, ২০২৩), Act No. XII of 2023: the standing
  statute; repealed the Income Tax Ordinance 1984.
  `https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/acts/Income_tax_act_2023.pdf`
- **`[ITA-2023-6Sch]`** Income Tax Act 2023, **Sixth Schedule**: Part 1 (exclusions from total
  income: gratuity 2.5cr, pension, provident/gratuity/pension-fund contributions) and Part 3
  (eligible investments for the Section 78 rebate).
  `http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/act-part-print-541.html`
- **`[S153]`** Income Tax Act 2023, **Section 153**: advance tax from motor-vehicle owners at
  registration/fitness renewal (+50% per additional vehicle; adjustable, not refundable).
- **`[FO-2025]`** Finance Ordinance 2025 / FY2025-26 (interim government).
  Prothom Alo: `https://en.prothomalo.com/business/local/4nttx2jgti`
- **`[NBR]`** National Board of Revenue: `https://nbr.gov.bd`
- **`[eReturn]`** e-Return portal: `https://etaxnbr.gov.bd`
- **`[TIN]`** e-TIN registration: `https://secure.incometax.gov.bd`

## Reading the official PDFs

The gazette and the NBR guides are Bangla PDFs set in legacy fonts, so their text does not
copy or search reliably. Read them page by page (as images if needed) rather than trusting
extracted text.

## Caveat tag

Figures marked **⚠️ unverified** were not confirmed against the gazetted law or the NBR
guide. They must be presented with the caveat to confirm against the official text. The few
places where the calculator rests on an assumption are listed under "Guardrails" in `SKILL.md`,
and the calculator reports the relevant ones in its own `notes` output.
