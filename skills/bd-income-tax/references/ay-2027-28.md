# AY 2027-28 to AY 2030-31: parameters legislated in advance

The **Finance Act 2026** fixed the individual rate card for **five** assessment years at once
(Schedule 2, Part 1). AY 2026-27 is the current year (`ay-2026-27.md`); the four years below
are already law but remain **subject to amendment by each later Finance Act**. `[FA-2026]`

> ⚠️ Treat any result for these years as the **best current estimate**, not a final filing
> figure, and say so in the answer.

## AY 2027-28 (income year 1 Jul 2026 to 30 Jun 2027)

Shares para Ka with AY 2026-27: **identical** slabs, thresholds and minimum tax. Use it for
"what will I owe next year after my increment" questions. Run with `--year 2027-28`.

## All four years

- Minimum tax: **BDT 5,000**, or BDT 1,000 for a new taxpayer.
- Net-wealth surcharge bands: unchanged (see `ay-2026-27.md`).
- The calculator assumes the standing rules of AY 2026-27 continue: salary exemption (one-third
  or BDT 500,000), the Section 78 rebate (3% / 10% / BDT 750,000), the filing windows, the flat
  rates on dividends and precious-metal gains, and turnover tax. ⚠️ These sit in the Income Tax
  Act itself rather than in the five-year rate schedule, so a later Finance Act can change them.
- `tax_calc.py` builds these years from the AY 2026-27 entry so they cannot drift, and marks
  each result `"status": "legislated-forward"`.
