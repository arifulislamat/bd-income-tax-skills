# AY 2026-27: parameters (primary year)

**Income year:** 1 Jul 2025 to 30 Jun 2026 → **Assessment Year 2026-27**.
**Governing law:** Income Tax Act 2023 as amended by the **Finance Act 2026** (Act No. 96 of
2026, gazetted 30 Jun 2026). Rates are in its Schedule 2. `[ITA-2023]` `[FA-2026]`

Every figure below was checked against the gazetted Act and the NBR Income Tax Guide
(Nirdeshika) 2026-27 `[NBR-Guide-2026]`, except where marked **⚠️ unverified**.

All figures are in BDT. Numbers feed `scripts/tax_calc.py`. Never hand-calculate.

## General individual slab `[FA-2026]` (Sch. 2, Part 1, para Ka)

| Income band | Cumulative upper bound | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| First 400,000 | 400,000 | 0% |
| Next 300,000 | 700,000 | 10% |
| Next 400,000 | 1,100,000 | 15% |
| Next 500,000 | 1,600,000 | 20% |
| Next 2,000,000 | 3,600,000 | 25% |
| Balance | n/a | 30% |

The 5% bracket of AY 2025-26 is gone: the schedule starts at 10%. The slab widths apply to
income **above** the taxpayer's tax-free threshold below.

## Tax-free thresholds by category `[FA-2026]`

| Category (`tax_calc.py` key) | Threshold |
|---|---|
| General (`general`) | 400,000 |
| Women; anyone aged 65 or older (`female` / `senior`) | 450,000 |
| Third-gender; persons with disabilities (`third_gender` / `disabled`) | 525,000 |
| Gazetted war-wounded freedom fighters; gazetted injured July 2024 fighters (`freedom_fighter` / `july_warrior`) | 550,000 |
| Parent or legal guardian of a person with a disability (`--disabled-children N`) | + 50,000 per child/dependant |

- The disabled-child addition goes to **one** parent only when both are taxpayers.
- These thresholds do **not** apply to non-residents, except non-resident Bangladeshis.

## Salary exemption (employment head) `[ITA-2023]` `[NBR-Guide-2026]`

Exempt the **lower of** (a) one-third of total salary receipts, or (b) **BDT 500,000**.

Not counted as salary at all: employer-paid treatment for heart, kidney, eye, liver and brain
surgery, artificial limbs and cancer (non-director employees); travel and daily allowances
spent wholly on duty; group-insurance premiums paid by the employer; and medical
reimbursements under a group insurance policy.

## Exempt end-of-service benefits `[ITA-2023-6Sch]` `[NBR-Guide-2026]`

- **Gratuity:** exempt up to **BDT 2.5 crore (25,000,000)** when received from a **government
  gratuity fund** or an **NBR-approved gratuity fund**. Any **excess is taxable** as employment
  income. *(`tax_calc.py`: `--gratuity`, with `--gratuity-unapproved` to drop the exemption.)*
- **Government pension** is exempt.
- **Non-approved / unrecognised fund:** the 2.5cr shelter does not apply by its terms. The
  calculator conservatively treats such gratuity as **fully taxable**. ⚠️ unverified: confirm
  the exact treatment against the Sixth Schedule before relying on it.

## Investment tax rebate: Section 78 `[FA-2026]` `[NBR-Guide-2026]`

The rebate is the **lowest** of:

1. **3% of total income**, excluding exempt income, reduced-rate income, partnership/AoP profit
   shares, and final-tax income;
2. **10% of total eligible investment** (Sixth Schedule, Part 3);
3. **BDT 750,000**.

The Finance Act 2026 cut item 2 from 15% to 10% and item 3 from BDT 1,000,000 to 750,000, so
this year's rebate is smaller than last year's for the same investment.

Eligible investments include:

- life insurance premiums;
- provident fund contributions (government fund; recognised fund, employer and employee);
- welfare fund and group insurance contributions; approved superannuation fund;
- deposit pension scheme (DPS) with a scheduled bank or financial institution, up to
  **BDT 120,000 a year**;
- government securities (including sanchayapatra), up to **BDT 500,000**;
- new unit certificates, mutual funds, ETFs and joint investment schemes;
- new listed shares, mutual funds or debentures;
- zakat fund contributions and donations to listed charities, hospitals and institutions.

Notes:

- **Late filing no longer forfeits the rebate.** The NBR guide's own late-return example still
  deducts it. See "Return timing" below.
- **Clawback:** cashing in government securities, unit certificates, mutual funds or ETFs
  before maturity makes the rebate taken on them payable as additional tax in that year.
- Discard any "15% of investment / BDT 10 lakh cap" figures for this year: those are the
  AY 2025-26 rules.

## Minimum tax `[FA-2026]` `[NBR-Guide-2026]`

- **Flat BDT 5,000** for anyone whose total income exceeds the tax-free threshold, wherever
  they live. It applies even when the rebate brings the computed tax to zero or below.
- **BDT 1,000** for a new taxpayer.

## Turnover tax on business or profession: Section 163(6) `[FA-2026]`

If the tax on business or professional income is **less than** the turnover tax, the turnover
tax applies to the gross receipts of the business, whether it made a profit or a loss.

| Business | Turnover tax |
|---|---|
| Tobacco product manufacturers | 3% of gross receipts |
| Carbonated / sweetened beverage manufacturers | 2.5% |
| Mobile phone operators, NTTN | 1.5% |
| All other cases | **1%** |

- New manufacturers: 0.2% for the first three years of commercial production.
- Not applicable to commission businesses, delivery-order (DO) businesses, money exchange, and
  buying and selling gold, silver, jewellery, gems or platinum.
- Turnover tax paid above the regular tax can be carried forward to the next year.
- *(`tax_calc.py`: `--gross-receipts`, optional `--turnover-rate`.)* The calculator compares
  the turnover tax with the slab tax attributed to business income in proportion to regular
  income. ⚠️ The Act does not spell out that attribution, so present a turnover-tax result as
  an approximation.

## Net-wealth surcharge `[FA-2026]` (Sch. 2, Part 2) `[NBR-Guide-2026]`

Levied on the **income tax**, not on the assets, based on net wealth in the IT-10B statement.

| Net wealth | Surcharge (% of income tax) |
|---|---|
| Up to 4 crore (40,000,000) | 0% |
| Above 4cr up to 10cr, **or** more than one motor car in own name, **or** house property above 8,000 sq ft in total | 10% |
| Above 10cr up to 20cr | 20% |
| Above 20cr up to 50cr | 30% |
| Above 50cr | 35% |

- "Motor car" here excludes buses, minibuses, coasters, prime movers, trucks, lorries, tank
  lorries, pick-up vans, human haulers, auto-rickshaws and motorcycles.
- No income tax means no surcharge (there is no minimum surcharge).
- The calculator applies the rate to tax after rebate.

## Environmental surcharge `[FA-2026]` (Sch. 2, Part 3)

Payable for **each motor car beyond the first**, collected at registration or fitness renewal.
The car with the lowest rate is the free one. It is **not refundable and not adjustable**
against any other tax. **Electric cars are exempt from 1 Jul 2026.**

| Motor car | Surcharge per extra car |
|---|---|
| Up to 1,500 cc | 25,000 |
| Above 1,500 up to 2,000 cc | 50,000 |
| Above 2,000 up to 2,500 cc | 75,000 |
| Above 2,500 up to 3,000 cc | 150,000 |
| Above 3,000 up to 3,500 cc | 200,000 |
| Above 3,500 cc | 350,000 |

*(Not computed by `tax_calc.py`.)*

## Return timing: Sections 170 and 174 `[FA-2026]` `[NBR-Guide-2026]`

The single "Tax Day" deadline is gone. For AY 2026-27 the price of filing depends on when the
return goes in. Percentages apply to the tax payable with the return (Section 173).

| Filed | Effect | `--filing-window` |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026 | Incentive: **lower** of 5% or BDT 25,000 off | `early` |
| 1 Oct to 31 Dec 2026 | Nothing added, nothing taken off | `standard` (default) |
| 1 Jan to 31 Mar 2027 | Additional tax: **higher** of 2% or BDT 3,000 | `late_q3` |
| 1 Apr to 30 Jun 2027 | Additional tax: **higher** of 5% or BDT 5,000 | `late_q4` |
| After 30 Jun 2027, voluntarily | Additional tax: higher of 10% or BDT 5,000 | `after_year` |
| After 30 Jun 2027, on a Section 212(1) notice | Additional tax: higher of 15% or BDT 10,000 | `after_notice` |

- `--filing-date YYYY-MM-DD` picks the window for you.
- A **first-time filer** may file up to the 30 June after the income year at the normal rate;
  the calculator adds no additional tax for them in the two January to June windows.
- A taxpayer abroad for study, deputation, leave or lawful work may file within 90 days of
  returning.
- Filing **after the assessment year** also loses most tax exemptions (salary exemptions and
  a short list of Sixth Schedule items are kept), and delay interest runs 50% higher. Those
  two effects are **not** computed by the calculator.

## Advance tax: Section 154 `[FA-2026]` `[NBR-Guide-2026]`

A taxpayer whose last assessed total income exceeded **BDT 1,000,000** (raised from 600,000)
pays advance tax in four equal instalments: 15 Sep, 15 Dec, 15 Mar, 15 Jun.

## Income taxed at a flat rate `[FA-2026]` (Seventh Schedule) `[NBR-Guide-2026]`

| Income | Rate | `tax_calc.py` |
|---|---|---|
| Dividends received by an individual | 15% | `--dividend` |
| Gain on personal gold, silver, jewellery, gems and other precious metals (not traders) | 5% | `--precious-metal-gain` |
| Benefit received from a developer for land (cash or flats), less the land's cost | 15%, payable in three equal yearly instalments | not computed |

Flat-rate income is part of total income but stays out of the slab schedule and out of the 3%
rebate base. Gold, silver, gems, coins, paintings, antiques and club memberships became
capital assets from this year.

## Other heads and rules `[ITA-2023]` `[NBR-Guide-2026]`

- **Seven heads of income:** Employment, Rent, Agriculture, Business or profession, Capital
  gains, Financial assets (interest/profit, dividends), Other sources.
- **Rent:** rent income = total rental value + "special rental income". Total rental value =
  annual value + any service/repair charge paid by the tenant, less a vacancy allowance
  (allowed only with electricity bills). Of any refundable or adjustable advance, 10% of the
  year-end balance counts as special rental income each year; non-refundable advances are
  spread over the tenancy or five years, whichever is shorter. The NBR worked example deducts
  a **25%** repair and maintenance allowance for a residential property.
  ⚠️ unverified: the commercial-property repair rate (30% in earlier years).
- **Financial assets:** bank and sanchayapatra interest is taxed at regular rates and the tax
  deducted at source is a normal credit (sanchayapatra interest is no longer final-tax income).
- **Capital gains:** `--capital-gains` treats the amount as regular-rate income. Gains taxed at
  special rates (for example long-held assets) need separate treatment. ⚠️ unverified rates.
- **Residency:** resident if at least 183 days in the income year, OR at least 90 days in the
  income year AND at least 365 days across the preceding four years. Residents are taxed on
  worldwide income; non-residents on Bangladesh-source income. **Non-residents other than
  non-resident Bangladeshis: flat 30%**, no tax-free threshold. *(Not computed.)*
- **Agriculture:** receipts less cultivation expenses. Where no books are kept, **60% of the
  market value of the produce** is allowed as cost (not for land let on a crop-share basis).
  A special additional exemption for solely-agricultural individuals of about BDT 200,000 is
  ⚠️ unverified. *(Not computed by `tax_calc.py`.)*
